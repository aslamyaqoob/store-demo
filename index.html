Delivery, returns and payment

Delivery

We deliver all over Pakistan through trusted courier partners: 2–3 working days in Karachi, 3–5 working days in other cities. Delivery costs Rs 250 in Karachi and Rs 350 elsewhere, and it's free on orders over Rs 3,000. We call or WhatsApp you before dispatch to confirm your order.

Returns

Changed your mind? Return any unused item in its original packing within 7 days. If something arrives damaged, send us a photo on WhatsApp and we'll replace it.

Payment

Pay cash to the rider on delivery, or by JazzCash or Easypaisa when we confirm your order. Prices in AED or USD are shown for convenience; orders are charged in rupees.

Questions people ask

Which cities do you deliver to?

All of Pakistan: Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Hyderabad and every other city and town our couriers reach. Cash on delivery is available everywhere.

Why do you confirm orders on WhatsApp?

A quick confirmation means your parcel leaves the same day and the rider knows exactly where to go.

Can I open the parcel before paying?

You can check the outer packing and the item count with the rider before you pay.

How do I track my order?

Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and we'll tell you where it is.
